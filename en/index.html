Starring our dog, Ohagi

Ohagi is a papillon-chihuahua mix: black head with a white stripe, big feathered ears and a curly white tail.

Her friends Shiratama, Kinako, Yokan, Dorayaki, Mitarashi and Omochi are all named after Japanese sweets — and so is the studio: ohagi and kinako.

About Ohagi and the studio

Our promises

So that families and dog lovers can play with peace of mind.

  • Safe to play

    Every game is rated 4+ and needs no account. Chiwa-Chiku RUN is free with no ads or purchases. Games with ads never show them during play.

  • Kind to dogs

    No dog is ever hurt. A dog that slips up is never scolded — it just looks a little sad, or grins. And please don't share the game's chikuwa with real dogs.

  • No personal data

    We never collect names or email addresses. See each game's privacy notes for details.

  • Open about AI

    AI helped with the dogs' 3D models (Meshy), some artwork (Google Gemini) and the code (Claude Code), all chosen and checked by the developer. The music is human-made.
